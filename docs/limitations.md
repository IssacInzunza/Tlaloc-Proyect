# Scientific and operational limits

Tláloc is an experimental personal application, not a certified warning system or a replacement for official local meteorological alerts.

- Radar detects echoes. An echo is not proof of rain at the ground; absence of echoes is not proof of no hazard. PrecipRate is an estimate and may include frozen precipitation or artifacts. RQI does not establish surface truth.
- NOAA MRMS is not global. Border areas can work only where actual pixels and quality support them. Provider domain bounds are broad selection hints, not coverage polygons. Alaska/Hawaii/Caribbean/Guam catalog support has not yet received the same real GRIB integration validation as CONUS; dateline and polar rendering need further field checks.
- No per-site NEXRAD Level II/III decoder or fallback is shipped. Those sources were researched and their public catalogs checked. They remain an extension behind the provider interface.
- Source cadence, publication delay, partial products and outages affect lead time. The software withholds stale, missing-target or insufficient-area forecasts. It cannot provide persistent monitoring while the local app/computer is stopped.
- Global phase correlation assumes broadly coherent motion. Opposing cells, large uniform precipitation fields, stationary weak patterns and deformation can defeat the registration/association. A clipped region cannot be assigned trustworthy centroid motion. These conditions intentionally produce many informational-only regions.
- New-echo classification means newly observed relative to sufficient earlier coverage; it is not a physical prediction of storm formation. Split/merge cases have no forced one-to-one trajectory.
- Footprints are resampled at 1 km; arrivals near a pixel boundary are uncertain. Growth, dissipation, terrain and changes in motion are not modeled by constant-translation extrapolation.
- Numeric intervals are limited to evidence-supported arrivals up to 30 min. Geometric intersections are searched through 60 min; longer results are kept uncertain without a precise interval. A scientifically calibrated 30–60 min forecast is pending.
- Confidence and interval widths are uncalibrated heuristics. No statistical rain probabilities or measured operational accuracy are claimed. The bundled example has no observable dry-to-wet target onset; its arrival error is correctly null. A diverse independent historical evaluation with longer future horizons and surface references is needed.
- Replay validates against sampled target radar values and tracker-dependent next-centroid associations. Its onset midpoint errors are conditional on compatible hits; they are not all-event skill scores. Replays are capped at 18 real frames and may skip observations during a 60 min selection. Missing frames are not filled.
- At most 100 largest segmented components are tracked; components smaller than 2 km² are filtered. Tiny/weak echoes may be omitted. The circle radius stays fixed until the user changes it; the processing margin is only 20 km.
- GDAL may unpack a full national field internally despite the small output crop. Memory and CPU costs can reach hundreds of MB and several seconds per observation on an ordinary CPU. No GPU is assumed; initial live loads can take longer than a minute on slow networks.
- One-process serialized analysis, SQLite and local file cache are intended for personal use. No authentication, multi-user scaling or public hosting hardening is supplied. Switching location while a download is running aborts the browser request but not the already running server work.
- Map tiles and Photon address suggestions depend on third-party availability/terms and mapped OpenStreetMap address coverage. A missing house/street is not fabricated. External search requires consent, transmits typed text and an approximate map center, and keeps only a short RAM cache. Last live-watch preferences remain browser-local. The app contains a small real offline radar replay example, but map tiles and address search are not offline.
- Audio needs a browser user gesture and runs only while the app is open. No persistent push notification or certified emergency sound is implemented.

Planned scientific improvements: per-region local motion/optical flow with consistency checks; annotated independent episodes; uncertainty calibration; real integration tests for every MRMS domain; optional site-specific NEXRAD with quality-aware beam geometry; richer event-level evaluation and better handling of large/clipped precipitation systems.
